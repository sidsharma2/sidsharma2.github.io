Research interests

Fuel–turbulence–chemistry coupling in aircraft combustors

How alternative-fuel properties and combustor design influence turbulent mixing, residence time, flame structure, and formation of NOₓ, soot/nvPM, CO and unburned hydrocarbons.

Combustor-exit flow and turbine aerothermodynamics

How hot streaks, dilution wakes, turbulence and compositional non-uniformity affect turbine boundary layers, secondary flows, loss, heat transfer, cooling and stage efficiency.

Multiscale validation of sustainable aviation propulsion

Transferring fuel-, combustor- and turbine-dependent effects from laboratory rigs to full-scale engines and mission-level environmental impact.
